Modes

Set the tone of your desk.

A mode is a bundle of comfort suggestions — lighting, breaks and reminders. DeskSense never changes your room on its own; it offers these and you decide.

How a mode works

Suggestions, not automation

  1. 1 You pick a mode

    Or a recommendation suggests one based on your readings.

  2. 2 DeskSense proposes actions

    Lighting, breaks and reminders are listed for your approval.

  3. 3 You apply or dismiss each

    Nothing changes in your room without your say-so.
