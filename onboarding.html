Step 1 of 7 · Welcome

A quieter, more comfortable desk.

DeskSense watches five room readings and suggests small changes. It never diagnoses anything, and it never changes your room on its own.

What DeskSense does

  • ✓Notices when you drift closer than your preferred range
  • ✓Flags light, temperature and humidity outside your ranges
  • ✓Reminds you to move after a long sitting block
  • ✓Learns patterns from your own readings, not from anyone else

What it does not do

  • ✕Use a camera, microphone or wearable
  • ✕Guess how you feel from your face
  • ✕Diagnose pain, vision or any medical condition
  • ✕Switch your lamp or fan without permission
