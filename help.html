Frequently asked questions

The things people ask first

Does DeskSense use a camera?

No. DeskSense uses an ultrasonic distance sensor, an LDR light sensor, a temperature and humidity sensor and an IR presence sensor. There is no camera, microphone or wearable anywhere in the system.

Does it know how I feel?

No. Feelings only ever come from your own check-in. DeskSense never guesses emotions from your face or behaviour.

Will it move my lamp or fan for me?

No. DeskSense suggests changes to lighting and ventilation and you decide whether to apply them. Adaptive controls are off by default.

Why did a suggestion appear?

Every card names the readings that triggered it and the range you set. A suggestion only appears when a reading sits outside your preferences.

Is this medical advice?

No. DeskSense offers comfort suggestions for your workspace. It does not diagnose pain, vision or any medical condition.

Where is my data stored?

In this demo, data lives in your browser only. Nothing is sent to a server. See Privacy & data for retention and deletion controls.
